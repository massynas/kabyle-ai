# Benchmark protocol

Cover everyday conversation, comprehension, Kabyle generation, Kabyle↔French and Kabyle↔Arabic translation, idioms, code-switching and spelling variants.

Native reviewers score meaning preservation, naturalness, contextual appropriateness, orthographic readability and instruction following from 1 to 5. Mark omissions, additions, meaning reversals and unnatural phrases. Preserve disagreement and multiple valid forms.

Freeze test examples before training. Evaluate base and adapted models with identical settings. Never train on test examples or add them to a retrieval index.
