# Data policy

Do not add books, articles, dictionaries, recordings, website dumps or conversations to training data until legal basis and permitted uses are known. Public accessibility does not imply permission for model training; translations may also be protected works.

Each example should retain its original Kabyle text, translations, context, region/dialect, register, orthography, alternatives, source and locator, license/permission evidence, validation status and reviewer notes.

Preserve source spelling exactly. Store normalized text separately. Do not silently collapse distinctions such as ɣ, ɛ, č, ḍ, ṛ, ṣ and ṭ. Record regional variants rather than treating every difference as an error.

Linguistic validation and rights clearance are separate. Do not include personal identifiers or private conversations in public datasets. Keep train/dev/test separate; freeze test data and never use it for training or retrieval. Retain provenance so corrections and takedowns can be handled.
